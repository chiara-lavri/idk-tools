Choose a ranking style

Round-robin — every item faces every other item exactly once, then stops automatically and shows you the standings. Thorough, but the total number of matchups grows fast with a long list.
Elimination bracket — like a sports tournament: lose once and you're out. Only about one battle per item overall, so it's fast. Items knocked out in the same round end up tied with each other.
Group stage — items are split into small groups where everyone in a group faces everyone else in that group. The weaker half of each group is cut, the rest regroup, and it repeats until a small final group settles the top ranks directly. Much fewer battles than a full round-robin, but everyone still gets a fair multi-way comparison instead of a single do-or-die match.
